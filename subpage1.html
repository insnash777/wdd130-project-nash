Overview

Moogles are small, mild-tempered creatures commonly depicted with small wings, soft fur (often pink or white), and a distinctive pom-pom on a thin antenna atop their heads. They are diminutive compared to humans and appear in many games with varying proportions and features.

Appearance

Moogles typically have a round or small mammal-like body, with features ranging from rodent- to feline- or marsupial-like. A moogle's pom-pom is a red ball of fur on a wiry antenna; they are sensitive about it being touched. Their wings are often stylized — sometimes dark or bat-like — providing a contrast to their otherwise bubbly nature.

Variations

  • Color: Moogles appear in many color variations for fur and pom-poms across different games.
  • Proportions: In Ivalice-set games, moogles have larger ears; in the Final Fantasy Crystal Chronicles series, they are rounder with shorter limbs.
  • Size: Sizes vary from under a foot to several feet tall, but they are usually small relative to humans.

Behavior & Abilities

Moogles enjoy eating vines and Kupo Nuts, which are a favorite food. They are generally industrious and intelligent; many playable moogles specialize in tinkering, gadgetry, and engineering. In some games they can fly — in earlier depictions they may inflate themselves and float, while in other titles they use their wings to fly short distances.

Language, Names & Society

Moogles are often associated with the word "Mog" in their names. Earlier games had them communicate only with the cry "kupo"; later titles portray them speaking human languages while often ending sentences with "kupo". In some Japanese versions, moogles use the pronoun "mogu" for "I". They can maintain separate societies and provide long-distance communication services like the Mognet mail service.

Trivia

  • In some games, moogles do not wear clothes, though in Ivalice they may be one of the world's primary races and wear garments.
  • The earliest games sometimes depicted moogles without their trademark pom-pom.
  • There are in-universe hints that moogles have distinct blood types and cultural differences across regions.
